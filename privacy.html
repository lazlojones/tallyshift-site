Privacy policy

Tallyshift Privacy Policy, effective 30 September 2026

Tallyshift ("the app") is made by Lazlo Jones, lazlojones@gmail.com.

What we collect: nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. The schedules, pay rates, notes and settings you enter are stored only on your device.

Purchases: if you buy Tallyshift Pro, the purchase is processed by Google Play. We receive only confirmation that Pro is unlocked. We never see your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Backups: if Android device backup is enabled, your Tallyshift data is included in your personal Google backup, which Google encrypts and stores under your account. Backup, calendar and report files you export are saved wherever you choose, and you control them.

Notifications: reminders are scheduled and shown entirely on your device.

Children: the app isn't directed at children under 13.

Changes: if this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date. If the app ever starts collecting data, we'll ask for your consent in the app first.

Contact: lazlojones@gmail.com
