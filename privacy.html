TallyShift

Privacy policy

Updated 17 October 2026. TallyShift is made by Lazlo Jones. Contact: dev2@gtspro.com.

Your records

Schedules, pay rates, notes, job-bag checklists, actual-time records, manual balances and settings are stored on your device in app-private storage. We do not operate a schedule cloud account or add advertising or app-usage analytics. Employer joining is optional, coming soon and unavailable.

Google Play purchases

Google Play processes Pro purchases and optional creator tips. TallyShift receives purchase information such as status and tokens to confirm and restore access, not payment-card details. Google's billing SDK and services handle purchase-related processing under Google's policies. The billing SDK can also send technical billing diagnostics to Google, including app, SDK and Android version information. The packaged app includes network permissions supplied by its Google billing dependencies. Tips unlock no features.

Backups and sharing

If Android device backup is enabled, supported app data may be included in your Android/Google backup or device transfer. Files you deliberately export or share go where you choose. Backups may include pay and notes; keep them private.

Reminders and deletion

Reminders run locally, subject to Android permissions and device restrictions. Clear Tallyshift user data in More or Android app settings deletes local app data. Exported and Android backup copies remain separate; Google Play purchases are not cancelled.

Children and changes

The app is intended for working adults, not children under 13. Material changes to data handling will be reflected here. Contact dev2@gtspro.com with questions.
